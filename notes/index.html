Notes

Things I've watched or read, with only the most important points.

Chris Williamson (Modern Wisdom) · Video · Added Oct 17, 2026

The Worst Thing A Man Can Waste

  • Obsession is a rare, non-renewable fuel; when you have a positive one, surrender to it instead of balancing it.
  • Discipline is friction accepted, motivation is friction reduced, obsession is friction inverted.
  • Today’s discipline is often the residue of a past obsession that fossilized into identity.

The Inner Fortress (on Schopenhauer) · Video · Added Oct 16, 2026

How to Deal With Foolish People Without Becoming Like Them — Schopenhauer

  • Foolishness is contagious; the real risk is that arguing with fools makes you behave like them.
  • If someone wants to win rather than understand, stop arguing; a short answer beats a long defense.
  • Before responding, ask: do they want to understand, will it help, and will I respect myself after?

Naval Ravikant (via Iron Will) · Video · Added Oct 14, 2026

The Modern World Is Designed to Destroy You

  • Reality is neutral; the world reflects your own judgments back at you.
  • The meaning of life has no single answer, so you get to make up your own.
  • Feeds, sugar, drugs and porn are engineered to addict you; the modern struggle is drawing your own boundaries.
  • Learn to be alone with your mind; meditation is working through your mental inbox.

Robert Greene on Modern Wisdom (Chris Williamson) · Video · Added Oct 14, 2026

“This Is The Most Important Subject I’ve Ever Covered” - Robert Greene

  • History repeats, so read history to put things in perspective and see the broad direction.
  • Context from history gives you distance from emotions that feeds and demagogues play on.
  • Life feels flattened by social media; awe (the sublime) pulls you out of your ego.
  • Awareness of your mortality drives the search for something greater than yourself.
