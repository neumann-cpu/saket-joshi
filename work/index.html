Selected work

Three quiet deep dives from shipped systems at X / xAI.

X / xAI · Transformer Omni · JAX

Omni recommendation model

Problem. Thousands of features, adversarial manipulation (gaming the algorithm), and no sequential understanding.

What I did. Worked on a zero-heuristic Transformer-based Omni recommendation model in JAX — including scaling-law and architecture ablations, plus custom sharding for large embedding tables.

Outcome. 100% rollout across multiple surfaces including For You; ~4% increase in user active seconds and >10% lift in core engagement metrics.

Industrial deployment at X (2023–) — further internals under company policy.

X / xAI · MaskNet · bias-corrected objective

Immersive video recommendations

Problem. Media-tab watch time was under-optimized under biased training objectives.

What I did. Owned the immersive video recommendation engine. Shipped a MaskNet model with a bias-corrected objective aimed at maximizing watch time.

Outcome. >50% gain in watch time within the Media tab.

X / xAI · evaluation methodology · Semantic-ID

Embedding evaluation & Semantic-ID

Problem. Difficult to systematically judge whether an embedding or content-understanding model is actually better for ranking — not just different.

What I did. Designed a hierarchical topic-tree evaluation for embeddings; benchmarked LLM/embedding models and prompting strategies; trained a Semantic-ID model on multimodal embeddings.

Outcome. ~45% error reduction on the evaluation versus prior approaches.

Related writing: How do you know an embedding is better?

Industrial deployment details at X are limited by company policy.
