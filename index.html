Selected work

X · 2023–

Omni recommendation model

Legacy feature-engineering pipelines limited quality and maintainability on major surfaces. I designed and shipped a zero-heuristic Transformer Omni model in JAX — architecture ablations, scaling laws, and custom sharding for large embedding tables.

Rolled out to 100% of users across multiple surfaces including For You; ~4% lift in user active seconds and ~10% in core engagement.

X · immersive video

Immersive video recommendations

Media-tab watch time was under-optimized under biased objectives. I owned the immersive video recommendation engine and shipped a MaskNet model with a bias-corrected objective aimed at maximizing watch time.

>50% gain in watch time within the Media tab.

X · evaluation

Embedding evaluation & Semantic-ID

Hard to systematically judge embedding and content-understanding quality for ranking. I designed a hierarchical topic-tree evaluation, benchmarked embedding models and prompting strategies, and trained a Semantic-ID model on multimodal embeddings.

~45% error reduction on the eval versus prior approaches.
