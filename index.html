Saket Joshi

Curious about ranking systems that stay simple enough to ship.

Some things about me

  • I was a Member of Technical Staff at X / xAI, working on large-scale recommendation systems.
  • At X, I shipped recommenders used across the product — including a Transformer model rolled out broadly on For You — and worked on video recommendations and how we evaluated embeddings.
  • I studied data science at Harvard (and cross-registered at MIT Sloan), after computer science at IIT Tirupati.
  • At Harvard I was a research assistant in the Culture, Cognition and Coevolution Lab, using NLP to study questions in psychology and human behavior.
  • I like turning messy, high-scale ranking problems into systems that are simple enough to train, measure, and ship.
  • I teach and mentor when I can — I was a teaching fellow for reinforcement learning at Harvard.

Some things I’m interested in

  • Recommendation systems, ranking and retrieval, and representation learning at industrial scale.
  • Evaluation, datasets and benchmarks — how you know an embedding or a model is actually better, not just different.
  • Markets and automated decision systems (side research).
  • Building things with tight constraints — the interesting part is usually the constraint.
